About Pokémon Lunacore

Pokémon Lunacore is a fan-made Pokémon adventure set in the California-inspired Inflamous Region. Challenge Gym Leaders, discover original Pokémon, and stop Team Midnight from corrupting the power of the moon.

Features

The Inflamous Region

Explore sunny beaches, deserts, mountains, and massive cities.

Original Pokémon

Meet Fakemon and regional forms found only in Inflamous.

The Lunar Type

Discover a mysterious custom type connected to the moon.

Team Midnight

Face an organization experimenting with moon fragments and Victini.
