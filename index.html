About Pokémon Lunacore

Pokémon Lunacore is a fan-made Pokémon adventure set in the California-inspired Inflamous Region. Travel through sunny coastlines, crowded cities, dry deserts, rugged mountains, and mysterious locations shaped by the power of the moon.

Build your team with familiar Pokémon, original Fakemon, and regional forms found only in Inflamous. Challenge Gym Leaders, uncover hidden areas, meet unique characters, and learn why moon fragments have begun changing Pokémon across the region.

Standing in your way is Team Midnight, a secretive organization carrying out dangerous experiments involving lunar energy. Their plans eventually lead them to Victini, whose power they intend to corrupt and control.

Meet Team Midnight

Team Midnight grunts patrol the Inflamous Region, investigate unusual lunar activity, and interfere with anyone who gets too close to their operation.
